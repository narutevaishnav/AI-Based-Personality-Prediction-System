Big Five Personality Model

OCEAN Personality Traits

Openness

Measures creativity, curiosity, imagination, and willingness to experience new ideas.

  • Creative Thinking
  • Curious Mindset
  • Open-Minded
  • Innovative

Conscientiousness

Measures responsibility, organization, discipline and planning ability.

  • Organized
  • Responsible
  • Goal Oriented
  • Self Disciplined

Extraversion

Measures confidence, sociability and enthusiasm.

  • Energetic
  • Outgoing
  • Talkative
  • Friendly

Agreeableness

Measures kindness, empathy, cooperation and trust.

  • Helpful
  • Compassionate
  • Cooperative
  • Trustworthy

Neuroticism

Measures emotional stability, stress response and anxiety level.

  • Emotional Control
  • Stress Handling
  • Calmness
  • Self Awareness
